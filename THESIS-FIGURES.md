# Thesis figures: audit map

Every figure embedded in the thesis, the file that is embedded, the
script that (re)generates it, and the data it reads. All figure files
live in `experiments/out/figs/` (PDF; PNG mirrors in `figs/png/`).
The underlying plot functions live in `experiments/src/plotting.jl`;
the scripts listed here are thin drivers around them.

Data inputs:

* **cells** — `experiments/out/tables/cells.csv` (shipped in this repo).
* **truth** — `experiments/out/truth/*.h5`; regenerate with
  `experiments/scripts/00_generate_truth.jl` (~5 min) or unpack from
  the Zenodo archive.
* **runs** — per-run HDF5 outputs + MoleWhacker iteration logs under
  `experiments/out/runs/`; unpack from the Zenodo archive or re-run
  the grid (`experiments/scripts/04_run_all.jl`, ~24 h).
* **story** — instrumented per-iteration snapshot runs under
  `experiments/out/runs_storyboard/`; in the Zenodo archive, or
  regenerated by `figs_storyboard.jl` itself (illustrative reruns,
  minutes; not part of the benchmark grid).

Notes.

* The full iteration-convergence set for **every** (problem, d, B)
  cell — plus `experiments/out/tables/mw_iteration_summary.csv`
  (thesis Table 8.2) — is produced by `tools/figs_iterconv.jl`;
  `tools/figs_storyboard.jl` re-renders the seven headline-budget
  panels with the final decade-tick styling.
* The shell/MW running-efficiency single panel (not embedded, part of
  the diagnostic atlas) gets its final annotation from
  `tools/figs_runconv_shell.jl`.
* The cross-algorithm efficiency overlays
  (`runconv__*__all__eta`, diagnostic atlas) come from
  `tools/figs_runconv_overlays.jl`.
* The remaining ~350 figure files in `experiments/out/figs/` are the
  diagnostic atlas referenced in thesis Appendix B (per-algorithm
  triangle plots at all dimensions, weight histograms, trace plots,
  mole maps, single-algorithm running-efficiency panels, hardness
  sweeps); they are produced by `scripts/03_plots.jl` and the tools
  above.
